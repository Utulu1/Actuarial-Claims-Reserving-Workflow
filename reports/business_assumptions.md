# Business Assumptions

## Client

Allstate Insurance Group

## Line of Business

Workers Compensation Insurance

## Objective

Estimate outstanding claim liabilities using historical claims development patterns.

## Assumptions

- Historical claims development is predictive of future development.
- Claims are reported consistently across accident years.
- No significant changes in claims handling practices occurred during the observation period.
- Inflation effects are assumed to be embedded within historical development.
- Claims development follows stable actuarial patterns suitable for deterministic reserving techniques.

## Scope

The analysis focuses on:

- Development triangle construction
- Chain Ladder reserving
- Mack reserve estimation
- IBNR estimation

This project does not include:

- Economic scenario modelling
- Inflation adjustments
- Stochastic simulation
- Reinsurance recoveries
